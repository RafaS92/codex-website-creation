### Class

#### Jikiden Reiki Seminar

Jikiden Reiki (直傳靈氣) means “directly passed down Reiki.” It is a traditional Japanese healing practice that has been preserved in its original form and passed down through the lineage of Chujiro Hayashi Sensei and the Yamaguchi family in Kyoto, Japan.

Unlike many modern Reiki styles, Jikiden Reiki remains close to the way Reiki was originally practiced in Japan. It emphasizes simplicity, hands-on healing, and trust in the body’s natural ability to restore balance.

The practice is gentle, non-invasive, and suitable for people of all ages. It can support physical, emotional, and mental well-being by encouraging deep relaxation and activating the body’s innate healing response. Jikiden Reiki is not intended to replace medical treatment, but it can be used as a complementary practice alongside conventional healthcare.

Whether you are seeking a natural approach to self-care, wishing to support your family, or looking to deepen your professional healing practice, Jikiden Reiki offers practical skills that can be integrated into everyday life.

Our seminars follow the official curriculum of the Jikiden Reiki Institute in Kyoto, Japan. Students receive authentic instruction, hands-on practice, and a deeper understanding of the philosophy and history behind this traditional healing art.

At Healing Garden, we share Jikiden Reiki with respect for its Japanese roots, while creating a warm and supportive environment where students can experience healing as they learn. Our intention is not only to teach techniques, but to help each person embody the simplicity and wisdom of Reiki in everyday life.
