# Client Discovery Summary

## 1. Project Overview

Nongnapat Neuman is a personal practitioner website for an established holistic healing professional offering in-person healing experiences in Chiang Dao / Northern Thailand. The website should introduce the practitioner, communicate the philosophy behind the work, explain available services and programs, and help aligned visitors feel safe enough to inquire.

The primary purpose is not aggressive selling. The site should build trust, create emotional resonance, and give visitors a grounded understanding of the practice before they decide to book, inquire, or join a Reiki training or workshop.

## 2. Business Understanding

Nongnapat Neuman offers holistic healing services rooted in energy work, somatic therapies, sound healing, nature-based healing, and mindfulness. The practice has more than 20 years of experience, which should be treated as a major credibility signal throughout the website.

The business appears to operate primarily in person, serving visitors who come to Chiang Dao / Northern Thailand for healing, retreat experiences, workshops, or private sessions. The work combines grounded professionalism with a calm, authentic, nature-connected approach.

What makes the practice different:

- A unique natural setting that supports rest, grounding, and reconnection
- A high-quality, professional experience with a 5-star level of care
- A calm and authentic healing style rather than a performative or overly commercial wellness presentation
- Integration of Eastern and Western perspectives, energy work, somatic healing, mindfulness, and nature-based practices
- Offerings that can serve both first-time healing clients and practitioners/students seeking Reiki training

## 3. Target Audience

The website should speak to people seeking deep rest, balance, and reconnection on physical, emotional, and energetic levels. These visitors may be wellness travelers, retreat guests, conscious individuals visiting Northern Thailand, or people already interested in holistic healing, Reiki, somatic work, mindfulness, and nature-based healing.

The audience may be experiencing stress, burnout, emotional overwhelm, nervous system imbalance, or a sense of disconnection from themselves. They are likely looking for a place that feels safe, authentic, professional, and inspiring.

A secondary audience includes practitioners, students, and spiritually curious visitors interested in Reiki trainings and integrative healing workshops.

Trust factors that matter:

- Clear explanation of services without vague or exaggerated claims
- Evidence of long-term experience and professionalism
- A calm, respectful tone that does not pressure visitors
- A strong sense of place and natural environment
- Clear contact, inquiry, booking, and workshop registration paths
- FAQ content that helps visitors understand what to expect before arriving

## 4. Website Goals

Main website goals:

- Build trust and credibility for Nongnapat Neuman as a real, professional healing practice
- Clearly explain the practitioner’s philosophy, services, retreats, and workshops
- Communicate the integrative approach across Eastern and Western healing, energy work, somatic healing, mindfulness, and nature
- Invite inquiries for private sessions, retreats, Reiki trainings, and workshops
- Support contact through WhatsApp, email, and a contact form
- Reflect the calm, safe, grounded, and nature-based environment
- Answer common questions so visitors feel prepared and reassured
- Attract aligned visitors and increase inquiry volume over time

Primary call-to-action:

- Contact / Inquire

Secondary calls-to-action:

- Join
- Learn More

The CTA language should feel gentle and spacious. The user should feel invited to take the next step at their own pace.

## 5. Brand Personality & Vibe

The website should feel calm, grounded, safe, professional, natural, and restorative. It should communicate depth and care without becoming overly mystical, abstract, or sales-focused.

Recommended tone:

- Warm
- Clear
- Reassuring
- Grounded
- Respectful
- Gentle
- Professional

Recommended visual mood:

- Nature-led and spacious
- Quietly premium rather than flashy
- Soft, organic, and breathable
- Rooted in the environment of Chiang Dao / Northern Thailand
- Clean enough to make services, retreats, and workshops easy to understand

The user experience should create a sense of arriving somewhere safe. Visitors should be able to slow down, understand the work, and move naturally toward inquiry without feeling pushed.

## 7. Recommended Website Structure

- Home: Introduce Nongnapat Neuman, establish the calming nature-based setting, summarize the healing approach, highlight key offerings, and guide visitors toward inquiry.
- About: Share the practitioner’s background, 20+ years of experience, philosophy, and integrative approach to healing.
- Services: Explain private healing sessions and modalities such as energy work, somatic therapies, sound healing, mindfulness, and nature-based healing.
- Retreats & Workshops: Present retreat experiences, integrative healing workshops, and Reiki trainings for guests, students, and practitioners.
- Reiki Training: If Reiki education is a significant offering, this may deserve its own page or dedicated section explaining levels, format, who it is for, and how to inquire or register.
- FAQ: Answer common questions about what to expect, how to prepare, who the work is for, location, booking, and contact.
- Contact / Inquire: Provide a simple contact form, WhatsApp link, email, location context, and clear inquiry paths for sessions, retreats, workshops, and trainings.

Recommended initial scope: A Compact multi-section site could work if content is limited. A multi-page site is preferable if retreats, Reiki trainings, and service explanations need meaningful depth.
