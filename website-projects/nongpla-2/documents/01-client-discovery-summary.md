# Client Discovery Summary

## 1. Project Overview

Nongnapat Neuman is a practitioner-led holistic healing practice offering in-person healing experiences rooted in energy work, somatic therapies, sound healing, nature-based healing, mindfulness, and Reiki education. The website should establish trust, communicate the depth and professionalism of the practice, and help aligned visitors feel calm and confident enough to inquire.

The site should feel less like a sales funnel and more like a grounded invitation. It needs to explain the philosophy, services, retreats, and workshops clearly while preserving a sense of spaciousness, safety, and emotional resonance.

## 2. Business Understanding

Nongnapat Neuman is both the professional practitioner and the business identity. The practice has been active for more than 20 years and operates primarily in person. Its services include holistic healing, energy work, somatic therapies, sound healing, nature-based healing, mindfulness, retreats, and Reiki trainings or workshops.

The practice is differentiated by its unique natural setting, calm and grounded approach, high-quality service standards, and a level of professionalism described as a 5-star experience. The work appears to combine Eastern and Western healing perspectives with body-based, energetic, and nature-connected practices.

Assumption: The business is likely based in or near Chiang Dao / Northern Thailand, since the ideal audience includes retreat guests and wellness travelers visiting that area.

## 3. Target Audience

The primary audience includes people seeking deep rest, balance, and reconnection across physical, emotional, and energetic dimensions. They may be experiencing stress, burnout, emotional overwhelm, nervous system imbalance, or a desire to slow down and reconnect with themselves.

Secondary audiences include wellness travelers and retreat guests visiting Chiang Dao or Northern Thailand, as well as practitioners or students interested in authentic Reiki trainings and integrative healing workshops.

This audience needs to feel safe before reaching out. They are likely looking for a practitioner who feels experienced, grounded, trustworthy, spiritually aware, and professional without being overly clinical or overly promotional.

Important trust factors include:

- 20+ years of experience
- Clear explanation of services and healing approach
- Calm, authentic, non-pressuring language
- Professional presentation and easy inquiry options
- Evidence of the natural setting and quality of the experience
- Clear answers to common questions before booking or applying

## 4. Website Goals

The main website goals are:

- Build trust and credibility for Nongnapat Neuman as a real, professional healing practice
- Clearly communicate the philosophy, services, retreats, and workshops
- Explain the integrative healing approach in a grounded, accessible way
- Help visitors feel safe, calm, and reassured before they contact the practitioner
- Generate inquiries for private sessions, retreats, Reiki trainings, and workshops
- Support contact through WhatsApp, email, and a contact form
- Answer common questions effectively

The primary call-to-action should be **Contact / Inquire**.

Supporting calls-to-action can include:

- Book
- Join
- Learn More
- Inquire about Reiki training
- Ask about retreats or workshops

## 5. Brand Personality & Vibe

The website should feel calm, grounded, spacious, authentic, safe, and quietly premium. The emotional tone should be reassuring rather than urgent. Visitors should feel like they are entering a peaceful, trustworthy healing environment before they ever make contact.

The brand personality should communicate:

- Deep experience
- Warm professionalism
- Natural calm
- Spiritual grounding
- Gentle confidence
- High-quality care
- Integration of body, mind, energy, and environment

The visual mood should likely draw from nature, stillness, organic textures, warm light, and spacious composition. The user experience should be simple, unhurried, and easy to understand, especially for people who may arrive feeling stressed or overwhelmed.

Assumption: The site should avoid flashy wellness trends, exaggerated spiritual language, or aggressive conversion tactics. A quieter, more mature presentation will better support trust.

## 7. Recommended Website Structure

- **Home**: Introduce Nongnapat Neuman, the healing philosophy, the natural setting, key services, and primary inquiry paths.
- **About**: Share the practitioner's experience, background, values, and approach in a way that builds trust.
- **Services**: Explain private healing sessions, energy work, somatic therapies, sound healing, mindfulness, and nature-based healing.
- **Retreats & Workshops**: Present retreats, integrative workshops, and special programs for visitors or groups.
- **Reiki Training**: Explain Reiki trainings, who they are for, what students can expect, and how to inquire or apply.
- **FAQ**: Answer common questions about sessions, preparation, location, booking, payments, retreat participation, and Reiki training.
- **Contact / Inquire**: Provide a calm, direct path to reach out through WhatsApp, email, or contact form.

Assumption: If the first website build needs to stay smaller, Home can include summarized sections for Services, Retreats, Reiki Training, FAQ, and Contact instead of creating every page immediately.
