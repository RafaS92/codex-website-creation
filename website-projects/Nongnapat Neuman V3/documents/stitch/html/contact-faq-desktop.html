Common Questions

How long is a typical healing session? expand_more
A standard session typically lasts between 60 to 90 minutes. This allows ample time for an initial grounding conversation, the healing work itself, and a gentle integration period before you return to your day.
How should I prepare for my first visit? expand_more
We recommend wearing loose, comfortable clothing and arriving 10 minutes early to settle into the space. Try to avoid a heavy meal or caffeine two hours prior to your session to ensure your body is in a receptive state.
Where is the sanctuary located? expand_more
Our primary sanctuary is nestled in the foothills of Chiang Dao, Thailand. We provide detailed directions upon booking to preserve the quietude and privacy of our visitors.
What payment methods do you accept? expand_more
We accept local bank transfers, major international credit cards via our secure portal, and cash for in-person sessions. Payment is typically settled at the time of booking to ensure your focus remains entirely on your healing during the session.
