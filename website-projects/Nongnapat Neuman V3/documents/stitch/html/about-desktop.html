A Journey of Silence & Skill

Nongnapat Neuman began her path as a healer in the temples of Northern Thailand, where she learned that the most powerful medicine is often the quality of one's presence. Over two decades, she has refined this intuitive foundation with rigorous certifications in Reiki, CranioSacral Therapy, and Somatic Experiencing.

Her approach is uniquely "quietly premium." She believes that holistic healing should be as professional as any modern medical practice while remaining deeply spiritual. Every session is treated as a sacred ritual of safety, where her clients—ranging from local residents to international seekers—find the space to unravel years of stored tension.

Today, Nongnapat operates from her private studio at the foot of the Chiang Dao mountains, offering a blend of hands-on bodywork and energetic alignment that respects the nervous system's pace.
