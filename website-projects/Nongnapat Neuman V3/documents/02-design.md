## Visual System

The approved design system is "Sacred Stillness." It should feel quietly premium, safe, still, grounded, wise, organic, and nature-connected. The design blends minimalist composition with tactile natural materials.

Use generous negative space, low visual noise, clear section rhythm, and tonal depth instead of sharp contrast or heavy decoration. The experience should feel like arriving at a calm healing destination in Northern Thailand, not a sales funnel.

## Components

### Navigation

Desktop navigation is calm and sparse. It includes the brand name, page links, and one primary inquiry CTA. Active pages use moss green and either a thin underline or bottom border.

Mobile navigation appears in the generated HTML as either a menu trigger or bottom/side navigation pattern depending on screen. The build should choose one cohesive mobile navigation pattern and preserve the visual language: large touch targets, moss active states, soft tonal backgrounds, and no clutter.

### Buttons

Primary buttons use moss green backgrounds with light text. Secondary buttons use sand or stone surfaces with moss text. Button labels often use uppercase label styling. Hover states should be restrained: slight background shift, subtle shadow, or small movement only.

### Cards

Cards should feel grounded rather than floating. Use tonal surfaces, soft radii, gentle shadows, and image-led layouts. Avoid heavy borders except subtle outline dividers where needed.

### Forms

Inquiry forms should feel calm and simple. Use soft backgrounds, clear labels, generous field spacing, visible focus states, and a single primary submit action. Contact should support WhatsApp, email, and form inquiry.

### FAQ

FAQ content should use quiet accordion or stacked question rows. Keep dividers subtle and avoid cramped answer blocks. The contact page should make FAQs part of the reassurance flow before or beside inquiry.

### Gallery / Image Controls

The retreats page includes previous and next circular controls around a gallery. If built, these should use accessible buttons with labels and maintain the soft border, moss text, and natural image emphasis.

## Imagery Direction

Stitch uses generated or placeholder imagery with strong natural-healing prompts. The implemented website should use available real assets if provided later, but preserve the same art direction:

- Chiang Dao misty mountains and limestone landscapes
- Healing room interiors with warm natural light
- Practitioner portrait with calm professionalism
- Hands, linen, stones, bowls, jasmine, handmade paper, bamboo, and wood textures
- Group circles in open-air pavilions or nature settings
- Singing bowls and subtle sound-healing details

Image treatment:

- Use large, real-feeling photography as first-viewport signals.
- Keep colors soft: moss green, warm stone, sand, charcoal, gentle morning light.
- Use object-fit cover, large rounded corners, and restrained grayscale transitions only if they support the calm mood.
- Avoid generic spa stock imagery, glossy wellness cliches, dark overlays that hide the setting, or purely abstract backgrounds.

## Interactions

Interactions should feel intentional and unhurried:

- Smooth color transitions on links and buttons.
- Gentle image scale on card hover.
- Optional grayscale-to-color image hover used sparingly.
- Active button states may use a slight scale change.
- FAQ accordions should open smoothly without layout jumps.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
