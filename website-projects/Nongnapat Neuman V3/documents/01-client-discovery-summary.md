# Client Discovery Summary

## 1. Project Overview

Nongnapat Neuman is a personal-practice wellness website for an experienced holistic healing practitioner based around in-person healing experiences in Chiang Dao / Northern Thailand. The website should establish Nongnapat as a real, professional, grounded practitioner with more than 20 years of experience, while helping visitors understand the philosophy, services, retreats, workshops, and Reiki trainings available through the practice.

The primary purpose of the website is trust-building. It should not feel like an aggressive sales funnel. Instead, it should create a calm, safe, and resonant first impression so aligned visitors feel comfortable reaching out at their own pace. The site should explain the integrative approach clearly, support inquiries through WhatsApp, email, and a contact form, and make the path toward private sessions, retreats, and Reiki trainings easy to understand.

## 2. Business Understanding

Nongnapat Neuman offers in-person holistic healing services rooted in energy work, somatic therapies, sound healing, nature-based healing, and mindfulness. The practice appears to serve both individuals seeking personal healing and people interested in structured learning experiences such as Reiki trainings and integrative healing workshops.

The business operates primarily in person, which makes place, atmosphere, and practitioner trust especially important. Visitors need to feel that the environment is safe, professional, restorative, and worth traveling to or including as part of a wellness journey in Northern Thailand.

Key differentiators include:

- A unique natural setting and healing environment
- 20+ years of practitioner experience
- A calm, grounded, authentic approach rather than a performative or overly mystical one
- High-quality, professional service delivery
- A premium, 5-star level of care and experience
- An integrative healing perspective that bridges Eastern and Western ideas, energy work, somatic healing, mindfulness, and nature-based practice

Assumption: The website should present Nongnapat Neuman as both a practitioner-led personal brand and a professional healing destination, rather than as a large clinic or commercial wellness company.

## 3. Target Audience

The website should speak to people who are seeking deep rest, balance, emotional grounding, and reconnection with themselves physically, emotionally, and energetically. They may be experiencing stress, burnout, emotional overwhelm, nervous system imbalance, or a sense of disconnection from their body and inner steadiness.

The audience likely includes:

- Wellness travelers visiting Chiang Dao or Northern Thailand
- Retreat guests seeking meaningful healing experiences
- Conscious individuals interested in mindfulness, energy work, somatic therapies, sound healing, or nature-based healing
- People who want an authentic, safe, and non-pressured therapeutic environment
- Practitioners, students, or spiritually curious learners interested in Reiki training and integrative healing workshops

Audience needs and expectations:

- Clear explanation of what is offered and what to expect
- A strong sense of safety, professionalism, and practitioner credibility
- A calm tone that does not overpromise outcomes
- Easy inquiry paths through WhatsApp, contact form, and email
- Enough information to decide whether the approach feels aligned
- A sense of the physical setting and the atmosphere of the experience

Trust factors that matter:

- Practitioner experience and background
- Testimonials or social proof, if available
- Clear service descriptions
- Transparent next steps for booking or inquiry
- Photos of the setting, practitioner, sessions, or retreat environment
- A grounded voice that balances spiritual depth with professionalism

## 4. Website Goals

The main website goals are:

- Build trust and credibility for Nongnapat Neuman as a professional practitioner and business
- Communicate the healing philosophy in a clear, grounded, accessible way
- Explain private sessions, retreats, Reiki trainings, and workshops
- Help visitors understand the integrative nature of the work: Eastern and Western, energy and somatic, mindfulness and nature-based healing
- Encourage aligned inquiries without creating pressure
- Support contact through WhatsApp, email, and a contact form
- Answer common questions before visitors arrive or inquire
- Increase inquiries for services, retreats, and workshops
- Establish a clear online identity for the practice

Primary calls to action:

- Contact / Inquire
- Book
- Join
- Learn More

CTA strategy:

The primary CTA should be inquiry-led rather than transaction-led. Language such as "Inquire About a Session," "Contact Nongnapat," "Ask About Retreats," or "Join a Reiki Training" will likely fit the desired tone better than urgent or sales-heavy language.

## 5. Brand Personality & Vibe

The website should feel calm, grounded, safe, authentic, professional, and nature-connected. It should communicate depth without becoming vague, spiritual without becoming exaggerated, and premium without feeling cold or overly polished.

Recommended emotional direction:

- Calm and restorative
- Trustworthy and experienced
- Warm but not overly casual
- Spacious and mindful
- Organic and nature-based
- Professional, clear, and grounded
- Subtle sense of premium care

Recommended visual mood:

- Natural photography of Chiang Dao / Northern Thailand, the healing setting, and calm session environments
- Spacious layouts with quiet rhythm and generous breathing room
- Earth-informed colors balanced with clean neutrals
- Typography that feels refined, readable, and human
- Minimal visual clutter
- Gentle transitions or interactions, if used

The user experience should feel like arriving somewhere peaceful. Navigation should be simple, section labels should be clear, and the copy should help people orient themselves without pressure.

## 6. Visual Reference Insights

No website references or inspiration links were provided in the initial questionnaire.

Strategic visual assumptions based on the business:

- Imagery should carry much of the emotional weight because the natural setting is a key differentiator.
- The design should avoid generic spa clichés and instead emphasize grounded nature, practitioner presence, care quality, and quiet professionalism.
- The site should feel more like a serene practitioner destination than a commercial wellness marketplace.
- If Google Stitch is used for design, the design direction should prioritize real environment, service clarity, trust-building sections, and soft but structured calls to action.

## 7. Recommended Website Structure

- Home  
  Introduce Nongnapat Neuman, the healing approach, the natural setting, core service pathways, and primary inquiry CTAs.

- About  
  Build trust through Nongnapat's practitioner story, 20+ years of experience, philosophy, grounded approach, and relationship to nature-based healing.

- Services  
  Explain private healing sessions and modalities such as holistic healing, energy work, somatic therapies, sound healing, nature-based healing, and mindfulness. This page should help visitors understand what they can inquire about and what kind of experience to expect.

- Retreats & Workshops  
  Present retreat experiences, integrative healing workshops, and group offerings. This page should support both wellness travelers and local/visiting guests interested in deeper immersion.

- Reiki Training  
  Provide a clear path for practitioners or students interested in authentic Reiki trainings. Include who it is for, possible levels or formats if available, expected outcomes, and inquiry/registration next steps.

- FAQ  
  Answer common questions around sessions, preparation, what to expect, location, language, booking, pricing or inquiry process, Reiki training, and whether the work is suitable for different needs.

- Contact  
  Provide a calm, simple inquiry page with contact form, WhatsApp, email, location context, and clear prompts for private sessions, retreats, workshops, and Reiki training inquiries.

Optional future pages:

- Testimonials, if enough strong testimonials are available
- Events / Upcoming Trainings, if dates and program schedules become a regular content need
- Journal / Reflections, if the practitioner wants to publish educational or reflective content over time
