## Coverage notes

- The screenshots are the full-resolution Stitch renders, not thumbnails.
- The generated HTML contains responsive breakpoints, mobile navigation patterns, layout classes, typography, colors, component styling, and interaction scaffolding.
- Stitch did not provide dedicated mobile screenshot variants for these seven screens. Responsive intent must therefore be read from the generated HTML and verified during implementation.
- Privacy Policy and Terms / Booking Policy are linked in the footer but were not supplied as screen-level exports.
- The Contact & Inquiry HTML contains a comment for a right-column inquiry form and JavaScript for form success/reset behavior, but the form markup itself is absent from the export.
- Remote image URLs embedded in generated HTML may be temporary. The full-page screenshots preserve their approved placement and crop, but production requires approved, licensed source assets.

## Authority boundary

These exports are authoritative for the final visual and layout system. They are not authoritative for business facts or operational details. Where generated copy conflicts with the approved discovery summary, the discovery summary controls.
