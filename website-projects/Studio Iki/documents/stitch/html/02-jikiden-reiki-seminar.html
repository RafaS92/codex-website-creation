The Curriculum

Shoden (Level 1)

Focuses on physical healing. You will learn the history, the concept of Byosen (finding toxic areas), the first symbol, and practical hands-on treatment techniques.

Okuden (Level 2)

Focuses on mental/psychological healing and distant healing. You will learn specific techniques for habit breaking and emotional blockages.

The Environment

Seminars are held in small, intimate groups to ensure personalized attention and ample practice time. The space is designed to be quiet, uncluttered, and conducive to deep focus and energetic sensitivity.

Global Certification

Upon completion, you will receive English and Japanese certificates directly from the Jikiden Reiki Institute in Kyoto, Japan. This credential is internationally recognized and signifies your training in the authentic lineage.
