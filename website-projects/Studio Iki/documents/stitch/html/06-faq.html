Seminar Questions

Please bring comfortable, loose-fitting clothing suitable for movement and extended periods of sitting. We provide all necessary props, including mats, cushions, and blankets. A water bottle and a notebook for personal reflections are also recommended.

Most of our introductory seminars welcome beginners with no prior experience. We clearly mark advanced seminars that require foundational knowledge. If you are unsure which level is appropriate for you, please contact us for guidance.

Therapy Questions

A typical session begins with a brief consultation to understand your current physical and emotional state. This is followed by the therapy itself, which may involve energy work, guided meditation, or somatic practices, depending on the modality chosen. We conclude with time for grounding and integration.

Important Medical Note

Our therapies and seminars are designed to support holistic well-being and are complementary practices. They do not substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you have severe physical or psychological conditions, please consult your healthcare provider before participating.
