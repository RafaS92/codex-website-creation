Jikiden Reiki

Jikiden Reiki is the original, authentic Japanese lineage of Reiki, taught exactly as it was by its founder, Mikao Usui, and his direct student, Chujiro Hayashi. "Jikiden" translates to "directly taught" or "passed down directly."

This practice focuses deeply on treating physical ailments and psychological issues by directing universal life force energy (Ki) to areas of accumulated toxins or stagnation (Byosen). It is a hands-on modality that is profoundly relaxing, often described as a warm, radiating glow that flows through and around you.

Biodynamic Craniosacral Therapy (BCST)

BCST is a gentle, non-invasive, hands-on therapy that engages with the deepest inherent healing forces within the body. It originates from osteopathic traditions but focuses on the "Breath of Life"—the subtle rhythmic motions expressed in all tissues and fluids.

The practitioner uses a light touch to listen to these rhythms, identifying areas of restriction, tension, or trauma held in the nervous system. By creating a safe, neutral space, the body is supported in its own self-regulation and structural realignment. It is particularly effective for stress, nervous system dysregulation, and chronic pain.
