The Practitioner

A Journey of Healing & Discovery.

For over two decades, I have dedicated myself to understanding the profound connection between the physical body and the energetic spirit, guiding others toward their own intrinsic balance.

Refining the Craft

My foundational years were shaped within the sanctuaries of world-renowned luxury wellness retreats, including Kamalaya and Four Seasons. Here, amidst pristine environments and discerning guests, I honed a practice that seamlessly blends ancient wisdom with refined care.

These environments demanded not just technical proficiency, but an intuitive presence—the ability to hold space for profound transformation within a setting of absolute comfort.

"Amidst the luxury, a persistent question emerged: How do we carry this profound peace back into the turbulence of everyday life?"

The Philosophy

"Healing is not becoming someone new; healing is returning to who we truly are."

My passion extends beyond individual therapy to the act of teaching. Empowering others with the tools to self-regulate and heal is the ultimate goal. True wellness is a practice, not a destination.

This ethos birthed the 'Healing Garden'—a conceptual and physical space designed for grounding. It represents the cultivated stillness required to listen to our own inner wisdom.
