## Visual Direction

### Design Intent

Create a quiet editorial sanctuary: refined, thoughtful, and human. The visual language should evoke a beautifully paced independent journal set within a restorative retreat. It must feel credible enough for professional education and warm enough for personal care.

### Layout and Composition

Use a restrained editorial grid with broad margins, narrow readable text columns, occasional asymmetric image-and-copy compositions, and deliberate shifts between intimate passages and expansive breathing space. Keep the seminar CTA visible but never loud. Use fine rules, measured alignment, and subtle section numbering to create orientation. Avoid card-heavy uniformity; let key stories unfold as editorial spreads.

### Typography

Pair an elegant, highly legible contemporary serif for display headings and selected pull quotes with a neutral humanist sans serif for body copy, navigation, labels, and forms. Use a disciplined type scale, generous line height, and modest contrast in weight. Do not use decorative calligraphy or pseudo-Japanese typography.

### Color Application

Let Porcelain dominate backgrounds. Use Plum Gray for primary text and strong actions, Warm Greige for quiet panels and dividers, and Soft Mauve or Dusty Lavender as sparing editorial accents. Keep large reading areas low-noise and verify all color combinations against AA contrast.

### Imagery and Graphics

Use generous documentary-style image placeholders with natural light, tactile detail, and honest environmental framing. Prefer quiet landscape crops and close human details over posed wellness clichés. Use simple typographic captions and no ornamental cultural motifs.

### Spacing and Components

Use generous vertical rhythm, fine borders, minimally rounded or square components, text-led service summaries, restrained accordion styling, and elegant form fields with clear states. Buttons should feel precise and calm, with a solid primary treatment and a simple secondary text or outline treatment.

### Navigation and Footer

Use a slim editorial header with a compact wordmark treatment, clear page links, and one composed primary CTA. On mobile, use a full-height but visually spare menu. Build a structured multi-column footer with a quiet closing statement, navigation, inquiry routes, location context, and legal links.

### Motion and Interaction

Use subtle fades, short vertical reveals, calm underline transitions, and restrained image scaling. Motion should clarify hierarchy, never create spectacle. Respect reduced-motion settings and keep all information available without animation.
