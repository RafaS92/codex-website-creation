# Client Discovery Summary

## 1. Project Overview

Studio IKI (息) is a holistic wellness and education practice led by Nongnapat Neuman. It offers authentic Jikiden Reiki training and a complementary range of personalised therapies from Healing Garden, a peaceful nature-based setting in Chiang Dao, Thailand.

The website’s primary role is to generate qualified inquiries while explaining the practice, establishing trust, and helping visitors choose between learning Jikiden Reiki and receiving a therapy session. Jikiden Reiki seminars are the commercial and mission-led priority. The experience should feel professional, calm, minimal, trustworthy, warm, elegant, and personal—like arriving at Healing Garden before the physical visit.

The site should communicate that Studio IKI combines more than 20 years of professional wellness experience, authentic Japanese Reiki lineage, gentle therapeutic care, and a nature-connected philosophy. It must be clear that these therapies complement rather than replace conventional medical care.

## 2. Business Understanding

### Business model

Studio IKI is a service and education business operating primarily through in-person seminars and therapy sessions. The questionnaire does not confirm whether any online teaching or remote sessions are available; this remains unresolved.

### Priority offer: Jikiden Reiki Seminar

Jikiden Reiki means “directly passed down Reiki.” Studio IKI teaches the traditional Japanese practice through the official curriculum of the Jikiden Reiki Institute in Kyoto. The seminars are intended for people who want practical skills for self-care, family support, or professional integration. The offer should emphasize:

- Authentic instruction rooted in Japan and passed through the lineage of Chujiro Hayashi Sensei and the Yamaguchi family.
- Official curriculum, hands-on practice, history, philosophy, and everyday application.
- A simple, gentle practice that trusts the body’s natural capacity to restore balance.
- A warm and supportive learning environment that respects the practice’s Japanese roots.
- No previous experience is currently stated as required; this should be confirmed before publication.

### Therapy services

#### Jikiden Reiki

A gentle, hands-on treatment delivered while the client remains fully clothed. It is positioned for stress relief, emotional balance, self-care, relaxation, and complementary support during a health or healing journey.

#### Biodynamic CranioSacral Therapy (BCST)

A gentle, non-manipulative therapy originating in British osteopathy. Using light, still touch while the client rests fully clothed, it supports nervous-system regulation, resilience, release of held tension, and deep restoration. It may appeal to people experiencing stress, anxiety, trauma, chronic pain, fatigue, headaches, or sleep disturbances.

#### Chi Nei Tsang

A Taoist abdominal therapy using mindful touch and breathing techniques. It is intended to support energy flow, circulation, organ function, digestion, release of abdominal tension, and processing of embodied emotional stress.

#### Crystal Energy Healing

A quiet energy-healing session combining selected crystals with gentle hands-on or hands-off work. It is intended to promote relaxation, reflection, clarity, emotional balance, and a sense of energetic harmony.

## 3. Target Audience

### Primary audience

Adults approximately 30–65, based in Thailand or travelling internationally, who value health, personal growth, natural approaches to well-being, personalised care, and authenticity.

### Seminar audience

- Individuals seeking authentic Japanese Reiki for self-care or family support.
- Wellness professionals, therapists, healthcare practitioners, and yoga teachers seeking skills they can integrate responsibly into their work.
- Business owners and people seeking a more balanced and meaningful way of living.
- Learners who value lineage, cultural respect, practical instruction, and credible certification.

### Therapy audience

- People experiencing stress, burnout, chronic tension, emotional strain, fatigue, or a need for restoration.
- People unsure which therapy is appropriate and who need calm, personalised guidance.
- Visitors who value a quiet natural environment and an unhurried, attentive practitioner relationship.

### Audience needs and pain points

- Clear explanations of unfamiliar or easily misunderstood modalities.
- Confidence that the practitioner is experienced, qualified, safe, and authentic.
- Help distinguishing Jikiden Reiki from modern Reiki styles.
- Reassurance about what happens during a session and whether previous experience is needed.
- Responsible clarity about using complementary therapies alongside medical treatment.
- Simple booking or inquiry steps, location information, and seminar details.

### Important trust factors

- Named qualifications and institutional affiliations.
- More than 20 years of relevant experience.
- Transparent descriptions of delivery and expected experience.
- Respect for Japanese lineage and origins.
- Testimonials from seminar participants and therapy clients.
- Authentic photography of Nongnapat, Healing Garden, teaching, and treatment spaces.
- Calm, grounded language that avoids exaggerated or guaranteed healing claims.
