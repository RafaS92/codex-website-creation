## Brand and Style

The brand personality is grounded, wise, and deeply serene, reflecting the “Refined Nature” of Northern Thailand. It balances the organic beauty of Chiang Dao with the five-star professionalism of a practitioner with two decades of mastery. The system is minimalist and tactile: generous whitespace and editorial typography are paired with subtle natural textures and earth-derived colors.

## Layout and Spacing

- Base spacing unit: `8px`
- Desktop content maximum: `1140px`
- Desktop gutter: `32px`
- Mobile horizontal margin: `24px`
- Desktop section gap: `120px`
- Mobile section gap: `64px`
- Desktop uses a centered fixed-width grid with expansive vertical rhythm.
- Mobile collapses to one column while preserving generous vertical breathing room.

## Shape and Depth

- Functional elements use restrained `4–8px` corner radii.
- Avoid conventional floating card shadows.
- Create depth through tonal layering, low-contrast outlines, and sparing ambient shadows.
- Organic image masks may appear as occasional brand moments, but functional UI remains structured.

## Component Direction

- **Primary button:** solid indigo, white Manrope label, approximately `4px` radius.
- **Secondary button:** forest-green outline with a very subtle fill on hover.
- **Cards:** no standard shadow; use warm neutral fills or low-contrast green outlines and at least `32px` internal padding on desktop.
- **Inputs:** bottom-border or quiet filled-field treatment; indigo focus state.
- **Navigation:** compact uppercase Manrope labels with generous gaps and a subtle active underline.
- **Testimonials:** large, centered, italic Libre Caslon Text with extensive whitespace.
