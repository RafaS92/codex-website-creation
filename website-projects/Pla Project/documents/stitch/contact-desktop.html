Gentle Preparations

How far in advance should I inquire? expand_more
For private retreats, I gently suggest reaching out 2-3 months prior to your intended dates. For individual sessions, a few weeks notice is often sufficient. However, space occasionally opens up sooner, so please do not hesitate to ask.
Do you offer introductory calls? expand_more
Yes, I offer a brief, complimentary WhatsApp call to those considering deeper engagements, such as a retreat. This allows us to sense if our energies align before making a commitment.
How do I reach the sanctuary in Chiang Dao? expand_more
Chiang Dao is approximately a 90-minute drive from Chiang Mai International Airport. I am happy to arrange trusted private transportation for you upon request, ensuring your journey is as smooth and peaceful as possible.
