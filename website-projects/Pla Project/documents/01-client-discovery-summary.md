# Client Discovery Summary

## 1. Project Overview

Pla Project is a professional portfolio and practice website for Nongnapat Neuman, a holistic healing practitioner with more than 20 years of experience. The website should introduce her philosophy, establish the legitimacy and quality of her in-person practice, and clearly present private healing services, retreats, workshops, and Reiki training opportunities in Chiang Dao and Northern Thailand.

The site’s central role is to build trust before a visitor arrives. It should create an immediate sense of calm, safety, and connection while giving potential clients enough practical information to decide whether Nongnapat’s approach resonates with them. Conversion should feel invitational rather than sales-driven, leading visitors toward a contact form, WhatsApp, or email inquiry at their own pace.

The strategic direction is a refined, nature-connected personal practice: grounded and authentic, yet polished enough to communicate a five-star level of care and professionalism.

## 2. Business Understanding

Nongnapat Neuman offers an in-person, integrative healing practice combining:

- Holistic healing
- Energy work
- Somatic therapies
- Sound healing
- Nature-based healing
- Mindfulness
- Reiki training and integrative healing workshops
- Retreat experiences

Her approach bridges Eastern and Western perspectives and combines energetic work with embodied, somatic practices. Rather than presenting these as isolated techniques, the website should explain how they support deep rest, regulation, balance, and reconnection across the physical, emotional, and energetic dimensions of a person’s experience.

The practice is differentiated by four connected qualities:

1. **Experience:** More than 20 years of professional practice provides a strong foundation for trust.
2. **Setting:** The natural environment of Chiang Dao / Northern Thailand is part of the healing experience, not merely a location.
3. **Quality:** Visitors should expect thoughtful, high-quality service and five-star professionalism.
4. **Presence:** The practice is calm, grounded, authentic, and free from exaggerated or high-pressure wellness claims.

The business currently operates primarily in person. The site therefore needs to help wellness travelers and retreat guests understand the destination, prepare for an experience, and make contact confidently from a distance.

## 3. Target Audience

### Primary audience

The primary audience is people seeking deep rest, balance, and reconnection while visiting or considering travel to Chiang Dao or Northern Thailand. They may be wellness travelers, retreat guests, or conscious individuals drawn to holistic, somatic, energetic, mindfulness, and nature-based practices.

They may be experiencing:

- Stress, burnout, or emotional overwhelm
- Nervous-system dysregulation or a sustained sense of depletion
- Disconnection from their body, emotions, environment, or inner life
- A desire to slow down, feel grounded, and reconnect with themselves
- Curiosity about authentic Reiki and integrative healing

They are likely to expect a calm, respectful, safe, and professionally held experience. Important trust factors include Nongnapat’s 20+ years of experience, a clear explanation of her methods, the quality of the setting, transparent session or program expectations, and an easy way to ask questions before committing.

### Secondary audience

Practitioners and students seeking authentic Reiki training or integrative healing workshops form a distinct secondary audience. They will need clearer learning outcomes, program format, suitability or prerequisites, schedule information, and a direct route to apply or register.

### Strategic assumptions to validate

- The website will serve both local/regional visitors and international travelers; final language, currency, and travel guidance requirements have not yet been confirmed.
- Some visitors may be new to energy work or somatic therapy, so plain-language explanations will be important.
- Testimonials, credentials, training lineage, affiliations, or other proof points may exist but were not included in the questionnaire.
- Accessibility, contraindications, and scope-of-practice language may be needed depending on the exact services offered and applicable local requirements.

## 4. Website Goals

### Primary goals

- Establish Nongnapat Neuman as an experienced, credible, and professional practitioner.
- Clearly explain the healing philosophy and the relationship between Eastern and Western, energetic, somatic, and nature-based approaches.
- Help visitors identify the service, retreat, workshop, or training most relevant to them.
- Create a felt sense of calm, safety, groundedness, and resonance before an in-person visit.
- Increase qualified inquiries for private sessions, retreats, workshops, and Reiki programs.
- Answer common questions so potential clients can take the next step with confidence.

### Primary call to action

**Contact / Inquire** should be the universal primary call to action, with WhatsApp as a prominent low-friction contact route and contact form and email as alternatives.

### Contextual calls to action

- **Book a Private Session** for visitors ready to explore individual support
- **Explore Retreats** for wellness travelers and retreat guests
- **Join a Workshop** for scheduled group experiences
- **Apply for Reiki Training** for students and practitioners
- **Learn More** for visitors who need education and reassurance before making contact

Calls to action should use gentle, choice-oriented language and avoid urgency or pressure.

### Success indicators

- A credible and cohesive professional identity online
- Growth in qualified website traffic from aligned visitors
- Increased inquiries through WhatsApp, email, and the contact form
- Increased interest in private services, retreats, workshops, and training
- Visitors arriving at first contact with a clearer understanding of the practice and what to expect

## 5. Brand Personality & Vibe

### Core personality

- Calm
- Grounded
- Authentic
- Safe
- Nature-connected
- Refined
- Professional
- Warm
- Spacious
- Trustworthy

### Tone

The written voice should be clear, reassuring, warm, and quietly confident. It should communicate depth without becoming abstract, overly mystical, or filled with wellness jargon. Claims should remain measured and credible. The visitor should feel invited to listen to their own needs rather than persuaded into a service.

### Visual mood

The visual direction should pair the organic character of the Chiang Dao environment with restrained, premium presentation. Natural imagery, tactile details, generous spacing, and an earthy palette can create a sense of place, while strong typography, consistent layouts, and polished photography communicate professional quality.

The design should avoid visual clutter, aggressive promotional devices, overly clinical presentation, or generic spa imagery. Nature should feel specific to the practice and location rather than decorative.

### Desired emotional and user-experience impression

The browsing experience should feel like slowing down: spacious, intuitive, unhurried, and easy to understand. Visitors should finish with the impression that Nongnapat is experienced, present, ethical, and attentive, and that the environment and services are held with care.

## 6. Visual Reference Insights

No external websites, moodboards, logos, screenshots, or image references were included in the supplied questionnaire. The current visual direction is therefore inferred from the client’s stated positioning and should be treated as strategic guidance rather than an approved design.

Useful directions for the Google Stitch design stage include:

- Editorial, image-led layouts with generous negative space
- A visual rhythm that alternates immersive nature imagery with concise, readable content
- Earth-derived colors informed by Northern Thailand and the actual practice environment
- Refined typography that balances warmth and professionalism
- Subtle motion only where it reinforces calm and orientation
- Simple, persistent access to inquiry options without making the interface feel commercial
- Mobile-first contact behavior, especially for WhatsApp

Before design approval, the client should ideally provide original photographs of Nongnapat, the practice setting, treatments, retreats, sound-healing instruments, and the surrounding landscape. Any logo or established visual identity should also be collected.

## 7. Recommended Website Structure

### Home

Introduce Nongnapat, express the promise of deep rest and reconnection, summarize the integrative approach, guide visitors toward the principal offerings, establish trust, evoke the Chiang Dao setting, and provide a clear inquiry path.

Suggested homepage hierarchy:

1. Hero message and primary Contact / Inquire action
2. Short positioning statement: holistic, somatic, energetic, and nature-based healing
3. Principal pathways: private sessions, retreats, workshops, and Reiki training
4. Introduction to Nongnapat and her 20+ years of experience
5. Explanation of the integrative approach
6. The Chiang Dao environment and its role in the experience
7. Trust content such as testimonials or credentials, if available
8. Common questions or preparation guidance
9. Final gentle invitation to inquire

### About

Tell Nongnapat’s professional story, experience, philosophy, values, and relationship to Eastern and Western healing approaches. This page should make the practitioner personally knowable while supporting professional credibility. It should eventually include verified training, credentials, Reiki lineage, and relevant milestones if available.

### Private Sessions

Explain the available one-to-one healing services in plain language, who they may support, how modalities may be integrated, what a session generally feels like, where sessions take place, and how to inquire or book. Avoid promising medical outcomes.

### Retreats & Workshops

Present retreat and workshop experiences, distinguishing current or upcoming offerings from custom/private inquiries. Include purpose, format, intended audience, setting, duration, inclusions, and the next step for each offering when those details are available.

### Reiki Training

Serve practitioners and students with information about Nongnapat’s approach to Reiki education, available levels or programs, learning outcomes, format, prerequisites, dates, and application or registration process. Exact program details still need to be supplied.

### FAQ

Reduce uncertainty by addressing what to expect, how to choose an offering, preparation, location, travel considerations, session length, booking, cancellation, accessibility, and whether prior experience is needed. Final answers must come from the client.

### Contact

Offer WhatsApp, email, and a concise inquiry form. The form should allow visitors to indicate their area of interest and share relevant timing or travel dates without requiring excessive personal information.

### Supporting legal pages

Privacy and website terms should be included as appropriate. A clear wellness disclaimer and cancellation policy may also be required once the service and booking model is confirmed.

## 9. Key Messages

### Primary message

Nongnapat Neuman offers a calm, grounded, and integrative path toward deep rest, balance, and reconnection in the nature-rich setting of Chiang Dao.

### Supporting messages

- More than 20 years of experience supports a professional, attentive, and trustworthy practice.
- Healing is approached holistically, bringing together Eastern and Western perspectives, energy work, somatic therapies, mindfulness, sound, and nature.
- The environment is an active part of the experience, offering space to slow down and reconnect.
- Private sessions, retreats, workshops, and Reiki training provide different entry points for individual needs and levels of experience.
- Visitors do not need to make an immediate commitment; they are invited to learn, ask questions, and take the next step at their own pace.
- High-quality care and authentic presence can coexist with warmth, simplicity, and groundedness.

### Messaging guardrails

- Do not make unverified medical, therapeutic, or guaranteed-outcome claims.
- Do not overstate credentials or Reiki lineage until the client supplies and verifies them.
- Keep language accessible to visitors unfamiliar with holistic modalities.
- Preserve a calm, invitation-led tone across all calls to action.
